Every conversation is in the inbox, also those the AI answers alone.

## Take over a chat

Write a reply. From then on the AI stays quiet in that chat, and it is yours. Press **Hand back to AI** to let it answer the next message.

Look at chats marked **Needs a person** first: the visitor asked for someone, a rule sent it to you, or the AI couldn't help.

## Get alerted

While the dashboard is open in a tab, it rings when a chat needs a person, and when someone writes again in a chat your team is handling. With the AI off, it rings for every message. The chat you are looking at stays quiet.

Click your name at the bottom left to switch **Sound alerts** on or off, or to turn on **Desktop alerts**: a notification that opens the chat. Both are remembered by that browser.

The organization's owner picks the sound, its volume, what to alert about and whether it repeats under **Settings › Alerts**, for the whole team.

> [!NOTE]
> Browsers play sound only after you have clicked somewhere on the page. Alerts cover the workspace you have open. To hear about chats with the dashboard closed, connect [Slack](slack.md).

## Replies and notes

- A **reply** goes to the visitor. It can have bold, italic, links and lists.
- A **note** is only for your team. The visitor never sees it.
- Files can be attached once you connect your storage under **Settings › Storage**: pictures, PDF, text, CSV, Word and Excel, up to 10 MB each and five per message.

## Close it

**Resolve** a chat when it is done, and **Reopen** it if needed. Someone who writes again after a day of quiet starts a new conversation.

## Who you are talking to

The panel on the right shows the visitor's email, country, current page and browser, and the chat's tags. To fill in more, tell the widget who your signed-in users are: [Widget in code](widget-api.md).

> [!TIP]
> Your team can also answer from [Slack](slack.md).
