## Add a teammate

1. Ask them to sign up with their email. They need an account first.
2. Open **Settings › Team**, enter that email and press **Add**.

Your photo shows next to your replies, in every workspace you belong to. Set it on the same page.

## Who may do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Agent** | Read and answer chats, write notes and articles |
| **Workspace owner** | Also change the AI agent, the widget, the team, integrations and the identity secret |
| **Organization owner** | Also the AI model and keys, the storage and the plan |

> [!NOTE]
> On your own server, sign-up must be open while a new teammate makes an account. See `SIGNUP_ENABLED` in [Self-hosting](self-hosting.md).
