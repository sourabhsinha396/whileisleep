**Visitors** shows who is on your site now, which page they are on, and who came by in the last 7 days. It updates by itself.

## Message someone first

1. Find the person. You can filter by name, email or page.
2. Press **Message** and write.
3. The message shows up in their widget, and the conversation is in your inbox.

## Names instead of "Visitor"

Visitors stay anonymous until they share their email, or until your site says who they are. See [Widget in code](widget-api.md).
